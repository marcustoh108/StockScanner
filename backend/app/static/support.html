How do I cancel my subscription?

Subscriptions are billed by Apple or Google, so cancellation happens in your device account settings, not in the app:

  • iPhone/iPad: Settings > [Your Name] > Subscriptions > StockScanner > Cancel Subscription.
  • Android: Google Play Store > Menu > Subscriptions > StockScanner > Cancel.

Cancelling stops future renewals — you'll keep access until the end of the period you already paid for.

Why is a metric showing "unavailable"?

Several metrics (insider buying, congressional activity, whale activity) come from free, public data sources that are sometimes rate-limited, temporarily down, or lag real-world filings. When that happens we show the underlying status ("unavailable," "stale") instead of guessing — try again later.
