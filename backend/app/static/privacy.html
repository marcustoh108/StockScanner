Information we collect

  • Account information: the email address and password you provide when you create an account. Passwords are stored as salted hashes — we never store your plaintext password.
  • Subscription/purchase records: when you subscribe through Apple's App Store or Google Play, we receive and store a transaction identifier, product ID, and expiration date to verify your subscription is active. We do not receive or store your payment card details — those are handled entirely by Apple/Google.
  • Usage data: the ticker symbols you look up and the (optional) list of existing positions you enter for the correlation feature, associated with your account, so the app can enforce fair-use rate limits and improve the product.
  • Technical data: standard server logs (IP address, timestamps, request metadata) for security and debugging, retained for a limited period.

How we use this information

  • To provide, maintain, and secure the App and your account.
  • To verify and manage your subscription status with Apple and Google.
  • To enforce rate limits and prevent abuse.
  • To respond to support requests you send us.
  • We do not sell your personal information to third parties.

Third parties involved in providing the App

To generate the analysis you request, StockScanner queries these third-party/public data sources on your behalf. Your ticker queries are transmitted to them as part of normal operation:

  • Yahoo Finance (via the yfinance library) — price, options, and earnings data.
  • openinsider.com — public SEC Form 4 insider-trading filings.
  • House Stock Watcher / Senate Stock Watcher — public congressional trading disclosures.
  • Apple App Store / Google Play — subscription purchase and billing.
  • Our hosting provider — to run the backend service that powers the App.

We do not control these third parties' own privacy practices; please review their policies separately.

Data retention

We retain your account and subscription records for as long as your account is active, and for a reasonable period afterward as needed for legal, tax, and fraud-prevention purposes. You may request deletion at any time (see below).

Your choices

  • Access / correction: email us to request a copy of the personal data we hold about you.
  • Deletion: email us to request deletion of your account and associated data. Cancelling your subscription is done through your Apple ID / Google Play account settings, separately from account deletion.
  • Cancellation: subscriptions renew automatically until cancelled. Manage or cancel at any time in your Apple ID (Settings > [Your Name] > Subscriptions) or Google Play (Play Store > Menu > Subscriptions) account settings — we cannot cancel it for you.
